Welcome, traveler! My name is Art—a digital-age samurai treading the path of computer engineering at Rajamangala University of Technology Isan (RMUTI), hailing from the land of Nakhon Ratchasima.


In this modern era, my keyboard is my katana, and every line of code is a stroke of the blade. From the very first line I wrote, I realized that programming is more than just logic—it is a "superpower." Much like a swordsmith who repeatedly tempers steel until it reaches a razor-sharp edge, I forge ideas out of thin air, strike down complex bugs, and build software to solve real-world problems. My training never ends; I am constantly seeking new technologies and tools to hone my blade, making it sharper and stronger than it was the day before.


Yet, a true warrior knows that strength must be balanced with a calm, steady mind. My favorite colors are blue and light blue—reminiscent of the vast, open sky—which help keep my mind cool and composed, even when tackling the most daunting bugs. When I sheath my sword to rest, you might find me watching a great film or listening to my favorite music, letting the rhythm refine my focus and clarity of thought.


Although my dojo is located in the heart of the Northeast, the spirit of a wandering samurai within me finds itself drawn to the mountains, the mist, and the cool breezes of the North—a tranquil sanctuary for the soul. And after a long, arduous journey, there is nothing quite like a steaming, rich, and flavorful bowl of khao soi gai (Northern Thai curry noodle soup with chicken) to restore a warrior’s strength!


My journey has only just begun. I am ready to take on new missions, collaborate with fellow developers, and harness the magic of coding to build something truly great in the digital world. Thanks for stopping by my corner of the web!
